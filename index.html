The Proof

Claim

The Sun is 99.86 percent of all the mass in the solar system.

In plain words: The Sun holds 99.86 percent of the solar system's mass. Everything else, every planet, moon, and rock, is the remaining 0.14 percent.

In simpler words: Almost everything in the solar system is the Sun. If you could weigh it all, the Sun would be 99.86 parts out of 100. All the planets together are just a tiny piece.
