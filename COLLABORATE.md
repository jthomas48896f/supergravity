# Collaborate on Atlas

Atlas serves one problem family: learning poverty and unequal
education. This page lists concrete, scoped ways an organization can work
with the project. Every task here is real and completable. Nothing on this
page implies an existing partnership; collaborations are invitations until
they actually happen, and no partner is named publicly without their
written agreement.

## Who this is for

School districts, state education agencies, and education publishers.

## Sized tasks

1. **Standards alignment (one subject, one state set).** Pick one atlas
   subject and one named set of state standards. We map the subject's
   topics to the standards and publish the mapping in the repo, with gaps
   listed honestly rather than papered over.
2. **Contribute a reviewed content module.** Author or sponsor one subject
   module in the atlas format. It goes through the same grounding policy as
   every other module: sources cited, reliability weighted, and it must
   pass the tutor eval self check before it ships.
3. **Run the tutor eval against your curriculum.** Bring your own question
   set drawn from your curriculum, run the harness in
   `tools/tutor-eval/` against it, and publish the comparison next to the
   atlas baseline. Your questions, your numbers, in the open.

## What you get

A public, dated record of the work: the mapping, the module, or the eval
report, committed in this repository with your organization's role stated
as you approve it.

## Start

Open an issue in this repository describing which task you are interested
in and the subject or curriculum you have in mind. Small and specific
beats grand and vague.
